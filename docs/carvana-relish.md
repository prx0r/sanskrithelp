# Carvaṇa / relish — felt-metrics vocabulary from Abhinavagupta's aesthetics

> Source: `tantra/abhinavagupta/aesthetics/abhinavabharati-gupta.txt`
> (Neerja Gupta 2017, on Abhinavabhāratī + Locana). Line refs below.
> This is the native replacement for imported valence theory in felt logging.

## 1. Sahrdaya: the qualified relisher (~1100–1115)
"Those people who are capable of identifying with the subject matter, **as the
mirror of their hearts has been polished through constant repetition and study
of poetry**, and who sympathetically respond in their hearts."
- Facility is TRAINED (repetition polishes the mirror) — aptitude ideology rejected.

## 2. Nirantara-carvaṇa: relish as mechanism (~1107)
"**Constant relishing [nirantara-carvaṇa]** of the subject matter that makes an
appeal to the heart is **the source of rasa**. The body is pervaded by it as
**dry wood is by fire**."
- Relish is not the reward after understanding; it IS the mechanism.
- Involuntary bodily signatures are objective markers: **romañca/pulaka
  (horripilation), sveda (sweat)** and the like.

## 3. Rasa arrives by suggestion, not analysis (~2620–2660)
"Rasa does not arise either through abhidhā or lakṣaṇā. It comes about through
**vyañjanā**, the power of suggestion peculiar to poetry."
- Aesthetic relish is "like the appearance of a **magic flower**; essentially a
  thing of the **present moment**, not depending on past or future."
- Design rule: suggestion-first presentation; analysis is scaffolding, never the taste.

## 4. Camatkāra = alaukika delight (~640–660, ~902, ~2812)
"Camatkāra or camatkṛti is another name for **non-worldly (aesthetic) delight**,
directly experienced by the sensitive reader." Wonder/mystic delight, completely
extraordinary — the word for what successful recognition feels like from inside.

## 5. Tasting vs analyzing (~788–800, śikhariṇī analogy)
Fastidious eaters given an unskilled sweet dish don't exclaim "how delicious!" —
they analyze: "the curds, sugar and spices were not properly mixed." Knowledge
of ingredients ≠ tasting. **Decompile drills and relish drills are different
faculties; log them separately.**

## 6. Beauty is bipolar (~793–800)
"It takes two, a subject and a relisher, to make beauty" —
subjective-cum-objective. Felt metrics therefore record BOTH poles:
the object's structure AND the subject's state. Neither alone is the data.

## Felt-logging schema this yields (phenomenology, not QRI import)
```text
valence       -1 … +1   (camatkāra present? alaukika quality?)
arousal       0 … 1     (sāttvika signs: horripilation, sweat, tears?)
clarity       0 … 1     (mirror polished: recognition immediate?)
tension       0 … 1     (analysis running? ingredients-listing?)
resolution    0 … 1     (magic-flower present-moment click?)
carvaṇa       0 … 1     (relish sustained? nirantara?)
```
Every label above traces to a quoted passage. QRI stays what it is: an external
heuristic for which geometries MIGHT feel resolved — tested against THESE metrics.
