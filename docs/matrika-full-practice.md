# The Full Mātṛkā Practice — as Abhinavagupta gives it (TĀ 15/109–146)

> Every step below traces to verse or apparatus. Gaps are marked [NOT GIVEN],
> not filled. Volume: `tantra/abhinavagupta/` (GRETIL + Dyczkowski vol 8).

## 0. Frame
- Trika rites are **heralded by three purifying depositions** (complete ~80490):
  **Mātṛkā** (feminine, normal alphabetical order), **Mālinī** (feminine,
  seeds mixed with matrices), **śabdarāśi** (male, fifty Bhairavas).
- Scope line (15.116): `mātṛkāṃ mālinīṃ vātha dvitayaṃ vā kramākramāt` —
  Mātṛkā or Mālinī **or both, in forward and/or reverse order**.
- Capstone (15.145): `śivatāpattir evārtho hy eṣāṃ nyāsādikarmaṇām` —
  **the point of all these nyāsa rites is becoming Śiva.**

## 1. Purify the hands (assumed knowledge)
"Normal basic procedure to begin by purifying the hands… Abhinavagupta does
not record the procedure, taking it for granted as well known" (~80505).
[NOT GIVEN: water, mantra, repetitions for the purification itself.]

## 2. Karanyāsa — install on the finger-joins
Thumb-to-finger contacts, each with a phoneme group (App. A):
- index: (opening mantras) · middle: (ta-varga group per extraction)
- ring + thumb: `eṁ taṁ thaṁ daṁ dhaṁ naṁ aiṁ anāmikābhyāṁ huṁ`
- little + thumb: `oṁ paṁ phaṁ baṁ bhaṁ maṁ auṁ kaniṣṭhābhyāṁ vauṣaṭ`
- rub backs of hands along palms: `astrāya phaṭ`.

## 3. Aṅganyāsa — six limbs, exact fingerings (vol 8)
- heart (index+middle+ring joined) · head (index+middle) ·
  tuft (right thumb) · shoulders (all fingers joined) ·
  three eyes (fore+middle+ring) · left palm/back + clap, then
  `yaṁ…kṣaṁ aḥ` on palm-back (astra).

## 4. Mātṛkānyāsa — the fifty, oṁ Xṁ + yoginī-name + locus
Mantras `oṁ aṁ śrīkaṇṭhāyai namaḥ` … through kṣa (App. A table).
Loci: TĀ 15.117–120 — `lalāṭavaktre…`, `skandha-bāhu-kara-aṅguli-nakhe`,
`kaṭyām ūrvādiṣu`, `pārśvayoḥ pṛṣṭhe jaṭhare hṛdi`, then the nine.
Project rule: verse-literal canonical; apparatus variant recorded, never mixed.

## 5. Mālinīnyāsa — na → pha order (MV 3/37–41 = TĀ 15/121–125)
Yoginī-name mantras (`oṁ sauḥ naṁ nandinī…`, App. B). Same 50 phonemes,
bhinna-yoni order. AFTER Mātṛkā (15.120: `ityevaṃ mātṛkāṃ nyasyen mālinyāstu`).

## 6. Śabdarāśinyāsa — fifty Bhairavas (App. C)
`rhrkṣamlvyūṁ`-prefixed formulas, same loci. Male assembly layer.

## 7. Close: argha → pūjā with installed sounds
15.146: `evaṃ nyāsaṃ vidhāyārghapātre vidhimupācaret… pūjayen
nyastavācakaiḥ` — after nyāsa, do the argha-vessel rite, then worship
**with the installed word-sounds**. The rite doesn't end at installation;
the installed body worships.

## 8. Scale kriyā to aim (15.143–144)
Siddhi-seeker: more outer kriyā. Dhyāna-japa practitioner: accordingly.
Mumukṣu (freedom-seeker): as desired. Effort is dosed by goal, not maximalism.

## Explicitly NOT GIVEN in TĀ 15/109–146
Pace/schedule · japa counts · breath ratios · posture/seat/direction/time of
day · 51-point finger choreography (see Nathas comparison) · hand-purification
words. Anything claiming these from TĀ 15 is invention — label it ours.
