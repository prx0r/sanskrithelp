# The Ladder of Sound (nādasopāna) — TĀ's own sound ladder

> Source: Dyczkowski's exposition reproduced in `tantraloka-complete.txt`
> ~36514–36640 (citing Dyczkowski 1992a:252-255, NT 22/21-22, VBh, SvT).
> Ascending order (practice climbs bottom-up):

1–3. **A-U-M** — the three audible letters of OṀ; waking / dreaming / deep sleep.
4–5. **Half Moon + Point** — withdrawal (saṅhāra): objectivity merged into
   subjectivity, then into consciousness beyond both. Point = Śiva-Śakti union
   concentrated; Half Moon = its first rupture toward manifestation.
6–7. **Sound (nāda)** — pulse (spanda) of supreme Speech (parā vāc); the
   manifestor (sphoṭa); aggregate of phonemic energies (śabdagrāma) generating
   the universe as Sound Absolute. Reverse view: resonance following the Point,
   condensing mantra-energy into Śiva's energy.
   - Unstruck form: **anāhatadhvani** — not individual phonemes but their
     unmanifest varṇa-energy at the Śiva-Śakti junction (~36584).
8. **End of Sound (nādānta)** — like the final bell-resonance just before
   silence; like running water with no physical sound. Entry into śaktitattva.
9. **Obstructress (nirodhikā)** — blocks lower gods from rising; also
   **mantrakalā**, where mantra-energy is most powerful. Obstacle AND floor.
10–12. **Energy (śakti) → Pervasive (vyāpinī) → Equal One (samanā)** —
    union of Śiva and power in three stages.
13. **Transmental (unmanā)** → Supreme Śiva.

## Anchor verses on disk
- `ghaṇṭānādavirāmāntāntamadhye paraṁ smṛtam` — "the supreme is known at the
  end of the resonance of bell-sound" (GRETIL ~14646; translation: mind merged
  in Space attains the supportless supreme state).
- OṀ stages: HAṀSA → bindu → nāda → nādānta → manifest Śiva (~14975–15000,
  via Dīkṣottara; SvT distributes six voids through the same ascent).
- Nāda-bhaṭṭāraka theology (~36096–36173): Nāda as Lord, Sadāśiva; the
  non-obstructed all-pervading resonance where nirodhikā is named.

## Design use (sonification ladder)
Each rung is a mix state: audible letters → withdrawal (lowpass closes) →
pulse/drone (nāda) → fading resonance (nādānta: bell tail + water texture) →
obstructive band (nirodhikā: mantra forward, lower gods muted) → expansion
→ silence. Grammar resolving = climbing, not brightening.
