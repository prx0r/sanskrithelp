## 1. Manifestation of sound (Ch.3, PDF pp.103–183)

**Sequence:** Śiva–Śakti (inseparable; Śakti = Word-aspect, vimarsa) → micro-grades
inside Śakti (unmanā → samanā → vyāpinī → śakti → nādānta → nāda → nirodhinī →
ardhacandra → bindu) → bindu (concentrated drop, śivaśakti-mithuna-piṇḍa,
mahābindu = AHAM) → threefold division (bindu = Śiva, bīja = Śakti, nāda = union)
→ triads (Vāmā/Jyeṣṭhā/Raudrī + Ambikā; icchā/jñāna/kriyā) → unmanifest
rava/śabdabrahman → **kuṇḍalinī in the middle of the body** → varṇa → speech.

> "Manifestation, as it is born out of the Word and along with it, may thus be
> understood as a flashing forth which gradually becomes obfuscated." (p.103)

> "The phoneme is a somehow unmanifest form of sound which shines in the rising
> of the breath [uccāre]." — Tantrasāra cit. (p.156)

> "Hamsa can neither be emitted nor held back, it is self-uttered and dwells
> within the heart of all creatures." — SvT 7.59 cit. (p.158)

**Two coincident kuṇḍalinī movements** (pp.142–164): spontaneous universal
emanation (mūlādhāra→navel→heart→throat = para→paśyantī→madhyamā→vaikharī) vs
deliberate yogic resorption. Vehicle is prāṇa/haṃsa; phonemes pre-exist
archetypally in bindu/triangle/cakra-petals as śabdarāśi/mātṛkā.

**Syllabus slots:** Audio 11–13 (uccāra: impulse→breath→sound→fading); Night
protocol step 8 (silence). The hamsa passage justifies "sound carried on
comfortable breath, repeated with awareness" (importantfirst.md) as traditional,
not invented.

**New for us:** the fine grades — nādānta (bell-after-vibration), nirodhinī,
ardhacandra (half-moon anunāsika) — as distinct silence-to-drop transitions to
refine our single "gap". Triadic machinery (Vāmā/Jyeṣṭhā/Raudrī, icchā/jñāna/kriyā)
we do not teach at all; candidate for Wheel II era, not now.

## 2. Levels of the Word (Ch.4, PDF pp.183–240)

| Level | What it is | Transition |
|---|---|---|
| **Parā** | Supreme Word = luminous pure consciousness (cit/vimarsa); `antarābhilāpa`, inner nod, not vikalpa; timeless ground, never left | tendency to manifest arises → paśyantī |
| **Paśyantī** | First cognition: wishing-to-know, not knowing; synthetic, desire for division; `ahaṃ` prevails over `idam`; potter's "I would make a pot" | desired division becomes actual language → madhyamā |
| **Madhyamā** | Division varṇa/pada/vākya achieved but mental only; inner speech; vacaka≈vacya superimposed (`sāmānādhikaraṇya`); mental/upāṃśu japa; "beyond breath-movement" | expressing/expressed separate → vaikharī |
| **Vaikharī** | Corporeal: condensed (`ghana`) state of the Word; phonemes + breaths + struck organs; only phonemic language is vaikharī (music/drums belong higher) | reversible: "going back from words up to the source" (p.236) |

> "When one speaks mentally to himself, or when one thinks very clearly in words
> and sentences, he is not in vaikhari but in madhyama." (p.230)

> "The Corporeal, indeed, is nothing other than a 'condensed' state of the Word." (p.236)

> "Thought is pure consciousness resting within itself, a condition where speech
> does not function any more, but is, nonetheless, still present somehow." (p.197)

**Syllabus slots:** Checkpoint 3 (silent Mātṛkā) IS the vaikhari→madhyama→paśyantī
ascent; Audio 02/05 (silence intervals) and the audio-gets-sparser logic now have
their textual spine. The interval (`unmeṣa`) between two cognitions (p.198) is the
scriptural analogue of our gap practice.

**New for us:** madhyamā mechanics (`sāmānādhikaraṇya`, covering-cloth) as the
*explanation* of why mental japa works; `uttara-mālinī` vowel-consonant mixing in
madhyamā as efficacy source (Wheel II material). Stotra-grade: gross madhyamā =
drum (distinct-indistinct); only phonemic language is vaikharī — our clips are
firmly vaikharī instruments, correctly so.

## 3. Phonematic emanation (Ch.5, PDF pp.240–347)

Mechanism: 50 `varṇaparāmarśa` — successive synthetic awarenesses, one undivided
vimarśa, timeless (not temporal evolution).

* **A**: first stir, avarṇa before phoneme (anuttara); contains kula in akula;
  a+ha inseparable; graphic body forms Devanāgarī A (pp.252–260).
* **Vowels = Siva-tattva**, 16 kalās: a divides (ānanda/visranti), i = icchā,
  u = unmeṣa/jñāna, ṛ/ḷ = sterile/void-pause (`śūnya`, binds nothing — cf. our
  clipless gaps), e = trikoṇa/yoni synthesis, ai = ṣaḍāra union, o/au progressive
  kriyā (au = triśūla-bīja, niranjana) (pp.261–288).
* **Bindu (aṃ, 15th)**: NOT a forward step — undifferentiated knower, holder of
  diversity, uccāra gateway. **Visarga (aḥ, 16th)**: bindu divided, step to
  differentiation; emission = pulsation itself
  (`visargamātraṃ nāthasya sṛṣṭi-saṃhāra-vibhramaḥ`, p.298).
* **AHAM (a+ha+ma)**: supreme emitting energy; "the resting of the light [of
  consciousness] within itself is known as the condition of 'I.'" (p.305).
* **KA→KṢA**: subordinate — consonants = coagulated vowels, wombs of Śakti;
  vowels give them life (p.311–312). Grammar-derived genealogy (ka from a,
  ca from i, ṭa from ṛ, ta from ḷ, pa from u, pp.313–317).
* **KṢA 50th**: kūṭa-bīja, pratyāhāra of all between; return pivot to anuttara
  (p.321). 50-not-49 is doctrinal rounding (p.248).
* **Level-relativity** (pp.322–346, tables pp.335–336): the SAME phoneme maps
  different tattvas at para (mahāsṛṣṭi: ka = earth upward) vs paśyantī (ka =
  Sadāśiva) vs madhyamā/Mālinī (na–ga = Śiva/Śakti support). Mālinī na→pha is
  REAL second configuration at madhyamā — mixed seeds/wombs (`bhinnayoni`),
  most efficacious for nyāsa; "everything is in everything" (p.342).

> "A is the original phoneme ... whence they all proceed and where all of them
> will return." (p.252)

> "The emittive state is the projection of the Self, in the Self, by the Self." (p.296)

> "The movements of emanation and of resorption are nothing but the emission of
> the Lord." (p.298)

**Syllabus slots:** Audio 08/09 (sṛṣṭi/apyaya as ONE visarga pulsation, not two
cosmogonies); Audio 14–15 (Mālinī as real second map — corroborated, proceed);
Wheel II ordering (Mālinī × locus × Śakti AFTER Mātṛkā stable).

**New/refining for us:** state WHICH level each frozen map belongs to (our v2 =
paśyantī/madhyamā nyāsa device; para maps run ka=earth — do not present ours as
the only map). au/e/ai weightings usable for Checkpoints 5/8 emphasis. ṛ/ḷ as
void-pause (not progression loci) — matches our clipless-silence treatment.

## 4. Sixfold course (Ch.6, PDF pp.347–388)

Two series of three — Way of Time (varṇa → mantra → pada: supreme/subtle/gross)
and Way of Space (kalā → tattva → bhuvana). Higher pervades lower; dissolution
runs upward (bhuvana→…→varṇa→Śiva). Whole course based on prāṇa (TA 6.21).

> "This whole sixfold course... is based on nothing but prana alone." (p.354)

* **Varṇa**: 50 phonemes, normally in **saṃhāra order kṣa→a** (liberation
  direction). **Mantra**: 10–11 bijas distributed in 5 kalās (TA 11 vs SvT
  systems differ — do not unify). **Pada**: portions/divisions (metaphysical,
  not grammatical "words"); 81-pada maṇḍala traversed counter-clockwise
  (resorption direction).
* **Kalā (5)**: nivṛtti (earth/feet) → pratiṣṭhā → vidyā → śāntā → śāntātītā
  (Śiva/Śakti/head). THE frame our pegs lack: kalās divide the body head-to-foot
  and everything else sits inside them (pp.377–381).

**Syllabus slots:** Checkpoint 6 (collective field) + T8 universe-into-body.
**Actionable delta:** install the 5-kalā head-to-foot frame FIRST, then
tattvas/bhuvanas inside kalās, then varṇa/mantra/pada inside kalās. Bīja
upgrade: after bare varṇa, add -M (KSAM/HAMSAM…) per TA 11. 81-pada maṇḍala
walked counter-clockwise = template for collective Checkpoint 6.

## 5. Mantra: definition, potency, empowering (Ch.7, PDF pp.389–466)

* **Definition**: "a mantra is a formula or a sound with fixed and prescribed
  form, to be used according to certain rules and in prescribed circumstances"
  (p.396) + efficacy acknowledged by its tradition. Highest mantras are beyond
  language, free from conventions (TA 5.140–141).
* **Nature**: "all mantras consist of phonemes and their nature is that of
  energy... this energy is matrka, whose nature is that of Siva"
  (Tantrasadbhāva via SSV 2.3, pp.391/402). "Cittam mantraḥ — the mantra is
  consciousness" (ŚS 2.1).
* **Mantravirya**: AHAM-sourced; "efficient power — in nature of heart — of all
  mantras. But for it they would be lifeless, like living being deprived of
  heart" (TA 4.193, p.405).
* **Empowering**: mantra never acts autonomously; needs utterance + prāṇa +
  proper pronunciation AND assimilation — prayatna, **anusamdhāna**
  ("incessantly inwardly aware of one's identity with it," ŚS 1.22),
  smaraṇa, uccāra (yogic, not mere pronunciation), bhāvanā, japa, dīkṣā/grace.
  Fusion: "along with devotee's mind, they achieve Sivahood" (SpK 2.1, p.410).
* **OM 11-stage spine** (pp.422–425): A (heart) → U (throat) → MA (palate) →
  bindu → ardhacandra → nirodhinī → nāda → nādānta → śakti → vyāpinī → samanā →
  unmanā (no duration). Body heart→brahmarandhra→dvādaśānta. Morae shrink
  (1→1/64); anatomy is imaginal ("inconsistent with human anatomy," p.424).
  Netra sequel: after dvādaśānta, amṛta tidal wave DOWN suṣumṇā (p.432) —
  missing in ascent-only stacks.
* **SAUH/KHPHREM pair**: sṛṣṭi-heart vs saṃhāra-heart (TA 4.186–191, 5.75–78);
  body route kānda→hṛd→kaṇṭha→tālu→dvādaśānta. "Just as big tree abides
  potentially in banyan seed, even so this world abides in 'seed of heart'"
  (PT 24, p.435).
* **Nyasa/divine body**: placings "suffusing it with the energy of the Word,
  since the body... must give way to a 'divine body'" (p.388); Netra mantras
  "converting yogin's body into 'divine body' [divyadeha]" (p.430).
  Prerequisite: identification first — "he cannot worship a deity who has not
  become god" (p.65). Mālinī "more especially to be used [TA 15] for the
  placings which infuse the adept with divine power" (pp.341–342).

**Syllabus slots:** OM spine = nightly ascent script (replaces generic cakra
run; ADD the amṛta down-flood). SAUH/KHPHREM = T8 heart pair. Anusamdhāna +
smaraṇa = checkpoint criteria (identity-attention, not repetition counts).
Bīja nasalization (-M) = post-bare-varṇa upgrade.

## 7. Honesty constraints (what we must NOT claim)

1. **No single-map literalism.** ST/KKV/NT disagree on correlations; cakra counts
   vary; allotments differ text to text. Our v2 is a CHOSEN recension
   (TĀ 15 verse-literal) — never "the Tantra says." (Ch.3 agent: pp.120–121)
2. **No Hz from Padoux.** Nāda/anāhata are explicitly inaudible/unstruck; Hz
   assignment would violate the chapter. BUT: do not cite him for banning
   frequency talk either — bell/drum/vīṇā resonances are valid markers (Ch.4:
   pp.218–219). Our ban stays hygiene-only.
3. **Colours: texts HAVE them** (white/red bindus, solar/lunar classes) as
   unstable dhyāna visualizations, not physics. "No fixed colours" = our
   pedagogy; "texts lack colours" = false.
4. **Pacing is ours.** Emanation is timeless/spontaneous; no textual metronome.
   Nightly counts and checkpoint ladders = modern apparatus, labeled.
5. **Efficacy = assimilation, not phonetics.** Proper pronunciation necessary
   but insufficient; dīkṣā/grace required at the limit; sadhaka-only path is
   the bubhukṣu (powers) track. Do not sell drill as liberation.
6. **No collapsing distinct systems.** TA vs SvT mantra counts, 118 vs 224
   bhuvanas, pada systems — present ONE table only as "this recension."
7. **Sequential practice is upāyic**, not cosmogonic fact (timeless archetype).
   Reverse runs are yogic re-reading, not a second cosmogony.
8. **Oral primacy**: writing/visualization subordinate to uttered sound for
   mantra proper (p.15) — our glyph-first drills are pedagogy for nyāsa, not
   the tradition's order. Say so.
9. **Mālinī transfer**: Mālinī keeps tattva order while scrambling phonemes;
   na–ga all = Śiva. Do NOT map Mālinī 1:1 onto Mātṛkā loci.
