# 1. Mātṛkā: construct the coordinate lattice

You've already found the correct entry point.

The ordered Sanskrit alphabet gets mapped onto the body. In TĀ 15 the vowels occupy the head/face, consonantal vargas distribute over limbs and trunk, and the final phonemes penetrate inward into bodily constituents.

So you learn:

**का? → shoulder**
**ma? → heart**
**ya? → skin**

until neither direction requires conscious retrieval.

This gives the body a **phonemic addressing system**.

Your phrase *peg system* is excellent pedagogically:

> The shoulder is no longer merely “shoulder”; it can instantly call `ka`, its sound and its articulatory structure.

But traditional nyāsa goes further. In related tantric descriptions, the deity-body itself is constituted by letters, and the practitioner installs this alphabet-body within himself. The Śāradātilaka explicitly describes Sarasvatī/Devīmātṛkā as possessing a body composed of alphabetic letters and immediately gives their nyāsa. [Wisdom Library](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/essay/isanasivagurudeva-paddhati-study/d/doc1457740.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

So:

**Bruno interpretation:** body loci = mnemonic pegs.
**Tantric interpretation:** phonemic powers = constituents of a divine body.

Both can coexist if we distinguish the layers.
