Yes. **Śiva Sūtras is probably the perfect first world** because it is compact enough to hold in memory, but conceptually rich enough to become the skeleton of the entire Trika system.

The three awakenings already give us a macro-architecture: Lakshmanjoo/Kṣemarāja present them as corresponding to **śāmbhavopāya, śāktopāya and āṇavopāya**—roughly moving from the most direct recognition of consciousness toward progressively more supported means involving mind, mantra, breath, body and related practices. [Lakshmanjoo Academy](https://www.lakshmanjooacademy.org/blog/what-is-real-posture-asana?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

So instead of one generic Bruno wheel, I think we build a **Śiva Sūtra concept machine**.

A sūtra such as:

**चैतन्यमात्मा — caitanyam ātmā**

becomes a node:

```text
SELF
↕
CAITANYA / CONSCIOUSNESS
↕
ŚIVA
↕
RECOGNITION
```

Then another:

**ज्ञानं बन्धः — jñānaṃ bandhaḥ**

adds:

```text
LIMITED KNOWLEDGE
        ↓
DIFFERENTIATION
        ↓
BONDAGE
```

Then:

**उद्यमो भैरवः — udyamo bhairavaḥ**

introduces another transformation:

```text
SUDDEN ARISING / UDYAMA
        ↓
ENTRY / RECOGNITION
        ↓
BHAIRAVA
```

So the sūtras gradually form a **graph of Trika primitives**, rather than 70-odd isolated quotations.

And that graph becomes a Bruno machine.

### One wheel could literally be the ontology

Centre:

**AHAM / awareness**

then rings like:

**STATE**
`limited ↔ expanded ↔ universal`

**COGNITION**
`vikalpa ↔ knowledge ↔ recognition`

**POWER**
`śakti ↔ mantra ↔ mātṛkā ↔ spanda`

**IDENTITY**
`aṇu ↔ self ↔ Śiva ↔ Bhairava`

**BINDING / LIBERATING FORCE**
`mala ↔ limited knowledge ↔ grace ↔ udyama`

**MEANS**
`āṇava ↔ śākta ↔ śāmbhava`

Rotate those while reading the sūtras and you're not merely memorising definitions: you're exploring **how Trika's conceptual system transforms**.

This is supported by the tradition itself being highly relational. For example, Lakshmanjoo's introductory material connects **Mātṛkā**, **svātantrya**, **prakāśa/vimarśa**, the **upāyas**, levels of speech and the **malas** as pieces of one account of consciousness and manifestation rather than isolated doctrines. [Lakshmanjoo Academy](https://www.lakshmanjooacademy.org/kashmir-shaivism/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Each sūtra should have four wheels

This is the structure I'd use.

**1. Sanskrit wheel**

```text
surface
→ sandhi split
→ lemma
→ morphology
→ syntax
→ sound/Mātṛkā
```

**2. Concept wheel**

```text
WHAT IS BEING ASSERTED?
identity
cause
means
state
transformation
obstacle
result
```

Then the actual entities involved.

**3. Hindi discussion wheel**

Listen to Mohanlal Gupta/Osho discussing the sūtra and identify reusable Hindi constructions:

```text
X का अर्थ है...
X को Y कहा जाता है...
जब X होता है तो Y...
इसका मतलब यह नहीं है कि...
इस अवस्था में...
```

**4. Practice wheel**

Not every sūtra needs to become an invented exercise, but where the commentary genuinely links it to practice:

```text
attention
breath
mantra
body
thought
recognition
effort
grace
```

And attach source/provenance so we can distinguish **what the text/commentary actually prescribes** from our own mnemonic machinery.

That distinction matters.

## Then the connections themselves become memory

This is the bit I particularly like.

Suppose you've installed:

**caitanya = consciousness**

That shouldn't end there.

The app asks:

> What does `caitanya` connect to?

You rotate:

**caitanya → ātmā**

Correct.

Then:

> What obscures that recognition?

You navigate toward:

**jñāna/bandha/mala/limitation**

Then:

> Which wheel concerns the energies producing differentiated experience?

Now:

**Mātṛkā / śakticakra**

Lakshmanjoo explicitly describes the master as one who rules rather than is ruled by the **wheel of energies (śakticakra)** in his discussion of Śiva Sūtra III.29. [Lakshmanjoo Academy](https://www.lakshmanjooacademy.org/blog/who-becomes-the-cause-of-inserting-knowledge-in-others?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

There is something wonderfully recursive there:

**we're using wheels to memorize a philosophy that itself talks about wheels of power.**

And unlike arbitrary mnemonic correspondences, the conceptual relations can remain source-grounded.

## The three awakenings can become three literal worlds

This could be beautiful.

### WORLD I — ŚĀMBHAVA

Sparse.

Almost no interface.

Concepts like:

**awareness**
**Self**
**Bhairava**
**udyama**
**recognition**

The UI itself becomes minimal because the teaching is about increasingly unsupported recognition.

### WORLD II — ŚĀKTA

Now language/thought becomes visible.

You manipulate:

**mantra**
**knowledge**
**mental construction**
**Mātṛkā**
**śakti**
**vikalpa**

This is naturally where **Bruno wheels explode into usefulness**.

### WORLD III — ĀṆAVA

Now the world becomes embodied:

**breath**
**body**
**mantra recitation**
**attention objects**
**practice**
**action**

Lakshmanjoo's descriptions explicitly distinguish āṇavopāya as involving supports such as body, breath, recitation and mantra, whereas śāktopāya works primarily through mental awareness and śāmbhavopāya dispenses even with that support. [Lakshmanjoo Academy](https://www.lakshmanjooacademy.org/blog/what-is-real-posture-asana?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

So the **interface could progressively change according to the philosophical method being taught**.

That's much better than merely making a prettier textbook.

## And the curriculum almost writes itself

I would now genuinely make:

**Śiva Sūtras = Semester Zero**

Goal at the end:

You can:

- recite/read the Sanskrit sūtras,
- write the core ones from memory,
- decompile their Sanskrit,
- explain the central ideas in simple Hindi,
- understand substantial portions of Hindi lectures about them,
- navigate the main Trika conceptual graph,
- identify which upāya/framework a teaching belongs to,
- connect each major concept into later texts.

Then **Pratyabhijñāhṛdayam** enriches the recognition/manifestation graph.

Then **Spandakārikā** adds dynamics.

Then **Vijñānabhairava** massively expands the practice operators.

Then **Tantrasāra** gives us Abhinavagupta's much broader compiler.

Eventually *Tantrāloka* isn't a giant alien book. It's a **huge elaboration of a conceptual world you've already been living inside**.

That's the strongest educational architecture we've hit so far.
