## 1. Aesthetics shelf (Abhinavagupta's own rasa theory)
- `tantra/abhinavagupta/aesthetics/abhinavabharati-gupta.txt` (325 KB) —
  Neerja Gupta (2017) on Abhinavabhāratī + Locana. Core native vocabulary:
  **rasa, carvaṇa (relishing/chewing), sahṛdaya (relisher), camatkāra**.
- Key line (~447): **music and dance are arts of pratibhā** (intuitive imagination).
  Art-making is classified under the same faculty our wheels target.
- Relish is inward: delight is "an inward reality in the relisher" (~810).

## 2. Camatkāra in the Tantrāloka (35 hits in translation)
- `svātmacamatkāra` — wonder of one's own nature (~1603); liberation as
  absorption in wonder-full I-ness (~8812, the jīvanmukti passage).
- `pratibhācamatkṛti` — wonder of intuitive imagination (~3384).
- Biernacki extracts (`tantra/matter-of-wonder/extracted-passages.md`):
  wonder as **recognition of subjectivity in matter**, not transcendent escape.
  Use this, not imported valence theory, as the native frame for felt metrics.

## 4. Dance / movement metaphor (textual)
- TĀ (~2770–2821): "the Self is the dancer" (Spanda doctrine, Bhāskara gloss) —
  consciousness dances by assuming every state. Movement-as-method is textual.

## 5. Sacred geometry with coordinates
- Trika Trident Throne, TĀ 15/297cd–312 (commentary on MV 8/55–68): square base
  (*catuṣkikā*, Pure Knowledge) **above the knot, below the three prongs**;
  locus given as Śuddhavidyā **between uvula and brahma-cavity**, with Sanskrit
  verses quoted in apparatus (`grantherūrdhvaṁ trīśūlādho…`). Body-as-diagram
  precedent with exact placement language.
- "Mandala" per se: only 1 hit (trident projection above). No separate mandala
  āhnika found on this volume.
