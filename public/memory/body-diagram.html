Start here — recommendation

Start with Mātṛkā ordered limb map · Night 1 = a + ā

TĀ 15 normally begins with Mātṛkānyāsa. MV 3.36 authorises Mālinī for śākta-śarīra when nyāsa has no special procedure — authorization, not a beginner mandate. Pathway Phase 6 adds Mālinī only after Mātṛkā is automatic (months 3–4).

Why not Mālinī first? Mālinī is a reconfiguration. Know what you are permuteing first. Night 1 na (śikhā) skips sounds you already produce; a/ā + forehead/mouth are precise touch targets.

Why not the green-core chakra map first? Pedagogical scaffold (tantrica2), not the TĀ limb body. Keep dual labels; never collapse into one doctrine.

Variations — same phoneme, different map

There is not one body chart. Sources hold at least three systems called “nyāsa maps.” Blending them collapses the coordinate you are installing. Pick one primary; overlay later.

Map A · Mātṛkā limb (TĀ 15 / MV 8)

Normal alphabetical order. Vowels on head/face. Vargas run down limbs.

Tripwires: Where does ka sit? Limb=shoulder · Mālinī=teeth · scaffold=root · batch=heart. Where is the heart letter? Mātṛkā=ma · Mālinī=pa · green-core=ta-varga row. Rule: one primary map per night. Dual-label later. Don’t “fix” one map to match another.
