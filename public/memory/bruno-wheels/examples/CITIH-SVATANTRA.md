# Trika micro-lab — चितिः स्वतन्त्रा विश्वसिद्धिहेतुः

IAST: **citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhihetuḥ**

Use this only after your basic sound map is stable.

## Six passes

1. **Raw sound** — shadow/chant without English.
2. **Boundary** — identify word boundaries and any sandhi.
3. **Morphology** — identify nominal forms and compound structure.
4. **Relation** — determine what is predicated of `citiḥ`.
5. **Concept** — only now introduce the philosophical reading.
6. **Memory world** — bind `citi`, `svatantrā`, and the compound as stable internal objects/relations.

The aim is for the Sanskrit sound to trigger the structure directly, not via English translation.
