## 0. Before the first letter — adhikāra and samaya

- **Ask, and let the asking qualify you.** Abhinavagupta composed the Tantrāloka
  because "requested by (my) sincere disciples and fellows" (ahnika-01 ~8900).
  A sincere request for the teaching is itself the opening move.
- **Receive the command as empowerment.** "To receive such a command is like
  receiving a descent of the power of grace *(śaktipāta)*… one is empowered,
  and so is given the authority *(adhikāra)*" (ahnika-01 ~8425). **[REC]**: take
  your resolve to begin as the command. You are not waiting for permission.
- **Take the vow of observance.** *Samayadīkṣā* is "initiation into the
  observance of the Rule" — it "introduces the neophyte into the religion and
  obliges them to follow its rules *(samaya)* but does not confer liberation"
  (ahnika-15 ~1275). *Samaya* means "'this should be done and that should not
  be done'" (complete ~63230). **[REC]**: write down your observance (what,
  when, how long). The content below is the *what*; the *when* is yours —
  the texts set no schedule (see §7).
- **Clear the threshold.** Obstacles strike even the engaged practitioner —
  "the Vināyakas conjoin him to transitory worldly pleasures" — hence the
  opening praise of Gaṇeśa (ahnika-01 ~8871). **[REC]**: begin every sitting
  with an explicit clearing gesture in your own idiom. The texts mandate the
  threshold; the gesture's form is yours.
- **Speech and secrecy.** The Devyāyāmala rules (TĀ 15/525cd–535ab, via
  complete ~78646): what is not to be said *(avāda)*, not to be done, kept
  hidden, worshipped, offered libation, served. Concretely: do not disparage
  the teaching, its teachers, or its practitioners; do not teach mantras to the
  uninitiated; keep the practice quiet ("people without sensible views… are
  not our own people," TĀ 1.193).

## 1. Hands first — karanyāsa

"By performing the deposition of the hands *(karanyāsa)*… the hands become
full of the energy of mātṛkā. By placing the consecrated hands on the
different parts of the body with the appropriate sounds, the whole body is
consecrated and transformed into mātṛkā." (TA15_NYASA.md frame, App. A intro.)

The hands are consecrated **before** they touch the body. **[REC]**: open each
sitting by installing the ten finger-mantras (App. A: thumb oṁ aṁ through
little-finger and palms, ~5652–5688) before any body locus. The instrument is
prepared before it is used.

## 2. The full traversal — Mātṛkānyāsa in textual order (āṇava stage)

TĀ 15/115cd–145 enjoins three depositions; "Mātṛkānyāsa normally begins"
(App. A). Base: MV 8/27–32. Traverse the fifty **in the table's order**,
sounding each mantra and touching its locus (Dyczkowski vol 8, App. A):

1. **Vowels, head and face** (mantras 1–16): topknot/mouth per the table —
   note the table pairs mantra 1 *(a)* with Topknot while TĀ 15.117 gives
   *lalāṭa* (forehead); both readings are recorded in TA15_NYASA.md §"Verse
   vs apparatus". Right eye, left eye, ears, nostrils (piṅgalā/iḍā), cheeks,
   teeth, lips, topknot *(aṃ)*, tongue *(aḥ)*.
2. **ka-varga, right arm; ca-varga, left arm** (17–26): shoulder, arm, then
   the verse/appartus split (verse: hand/fingers/nails, TĀ 15.118
   *skandha-bāhu-kara-aṅguli-nakhe*; apparatus: elbow/wrist/fingers).
   TA15_NYASA.md records both — use one per sitting, never mix maps on the
   same night.
3. **ṭa-varga right leg, ta-varga left leg** (27–36): buttock/hip *(kaṭi)*,
   thigh, knee, shank, toes.
4. **pa-varga, torso** (37–41): two sides, back, belly, **heart** *(ma)* —
   "pavargaṃ pārśvayoḥ pṛṣṭhe jaṭhare hṛdi" (TĀ 15.119).
5. **The nine deep constituents** (42–50): skin, blood, flesh, sūtra (sinews), bone,
   marrow, generative essence, breath *(prāṇa)*, generative — "atho nava:
   tvak-rakta-māṃsa…" (TĀ 15.119–120).

Method for this stage is **āṇava upāya** exactly as defined, TĀ 1.170:

> *uccāra-karaṇa-dhyāna-varṇa-sthāna-prakalpanaiḥ yo bhavet sa samāveśaḥ
> samyag āṇava ucyate* — "the penetration that takes place by utterance
> *(uccāra)*, the instruments *(karaṇa)*, visualization *(dhyāna)*, and the
> cycling of phonemes through their stations *(varṇa-sthāna-kalpanā)* is
> properly called Empowered-by-Atom *(āṇava)*." (GRETIL AbhT_1.170)

Sound it, breathe it upward *(uccāra* is "upward movement" of breath and sonic
energy, 1/~25640n), see it, place it. All four together — that is the textual
instruction, and it is also the wheel's WHEEL → GLYPH → MOUTH → SOUND → BODY
loop with classical names.

**Repetition rule**, TĀ 4.7: *bhūyo bhūyo vimṛśatāṃ jāyate tatsphuṭātmatā* —
"Bhairava's radiant power becomes fully clear to those who reflect upon it
**again and again**." Stay at this stage until each phoneme fires glyph +
sound + locus without searching. No pace is set by the texts (§7).

## 3. Consolidation — the compressed aṅganyāsa (TĀ 15/117–120)

The same āhnika gives the limb formula for rapid install after the slow work
(as recorded in TA15_NYASA.md from App. A — verse-source in TĀ unverified,
quote exactly as documented):

| Locus | Formula |
|-------|---------|
| Heart | `aṅ kaṁ khaṁ ghaṁ ṅaṁ āṁ hr̥dayāya namaḥ` |
| Head | `iṁ caṁ chaṁ jaṁ jhaṁ ñaṁ īṁ śirase svāhā` |
| Shoulders | `eṁ taṁ thaṁ daṁ dhaṁ naṁ aiṁ kavacāya hūṃ` |
| Three eyes | `oṁ paṁ phaṁ baṁ bhaṁ maṁ auṁ netratrayāya vauṣaṭ` |
| Hands | `arṁ yaṁ raṁ laṁ vaṁ śaṁ ṣaṁ haṁ laṁ kṣaṁ aḥ karatalapṛṣṭhābhyām astrāya phaṭ` |

Caution: the Heart string as documented omits *gaṁ* — probable doc typo
(ka-varga has five members), flagged, not silently repaired. Verify against
App. A before vocal use.

**[REC]**: use this as the consolidation pass — full fifty first, then this
five-locus compression in the same sitting. The texts give both forms; the
slow-before-compressed ordering is my recommendation, not their instruction.

## 4. Mind alone — śākta stage (TĀ 1.169)

> *uccāra-rahitaṃ vastu cetasaiva vicintayan yaṃ samāveśam āpnoti śāktaḥ
> so 'trābhidhīyate* — "the penetration one attains whilst contemplating
> reality with the mind alone, **without utterance**, is here called Empowered
> *(śākta)*." (GRETIL AbhT_1.169)

When the fifty are automatic, drop the voice, then the visualization: run the
identical traversal silently, loci only. The object is the same; the support
is subtler. The verse itself defines the graduation criterion — no external
threshold is given.

## 5. No thought — śāmbhava rest (TĀ 1.168; TĀ 4.2–7)

> *akiñcicintakasyaiva guruṇā pratibodhataḥ utpadyate ya āveśaḥ śāmbhavo
> 'sāvudīritaḥ* — "the penetration that arises for one who **does not think
> of anything**, by intense awakening, is said to be Śāmbhava." (GRETIL
> AbhT_1.168)

Purified construction resolves into the unconstructed: "consciousness with
thought constructs manifests radiantly as consciousness free of thought
constructs *(avikalpasaṃvit)* by the power of each particular purification"
(ahnika-04 ~642, on TĀ 4.2–7). Then, TĀ 4.207–208: in the mirror of vikalpa,
seeing oneself as Bhairava, "one swiftly becomes of that very nature."

## 6. Mālinī, then śabdarāśi — textual sequence

Only after Mātṛkā: "ityeṣa mātṛkānyāso mālinyāstu nirūpyate" — "such is the
Mātṛkā deposition; now the Mālinī is taught" (TĀ 15.120). Mālinī order from
MV 3/37–41, reproduced TĀ 15/121–125ab: *na ṛ ṝ ḷ ḹ tha ca dha ī ṇa u ū ba ka
kha ga gha ṅa i a va bha ya ḍa ḍha ṭha jha ña ja ra ṭa pa cha la ā sa aḥ ha ṣa
kṣa ma śa aṃ ta e ai o au da pha* (nādiphāntā — na to pha). Then the third
deposition, śabdarāśinyāsa (App. C). Do not begin Mālinī while Mātṛkā still
needs searching — **[REC]** test: fifty loci cold, no hesitation.

## 7. What the texts do NOT say (do not smuggle these in)

- **No pace.** Nothing about "two per night," nights at all, or any schedule.
  *Nitya* (daily) obligation yes; curriculum pacing no. Any number is modern
  pedagogy — label it.
- **No breath ratios.** TĀ 4.91's breath rule is negative: "prāṇāyāmo na
  kartavyaḥ śarīraṃ yena pīḍyate" — do not torment the body. No 1:4:2, no
  counts. Those come from later/living teachers, not this text.
- **No Hz, no colors, no imagery mandates.** The apparatus gives loci and
  deity-names, not visualizations. (Our body's color fields, where used, are
  marked PEDAGOGICAL.)
- **No TĀ-5 ladder on disk.** A seven-step *sthāna-kalpanā → … →
  akiñcid-cintana* progression is quoted online from TĀ 5; neither term
  occurs in our volume files (checked: zero hits). Do not cite it as
  Abhinavagupta until verified against Sanskrit.
- **No solo-practice clause.** The rite assumes teacher, initiation, lineage.
  What covers you: grace as sole cause; *ājñā*-as-*adhikāra*; the gradual
  *(manda)* descent through stages and teachers; the Kiraṇa ladder
  (teacher < scripture < one's own insight). That is textual comfort, not
  textual permission — read it as such.
