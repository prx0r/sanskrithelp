PROVENANCE — READ BEFORE MEMORISING

Two Abhinavagupta-tradition readings — kept separate, never merged. Verse (TĀ 15.118, GRETIL): dakṣānyayoḥ skandha-bāhu-kara-aṅguli-nakhe kacau vargau — shoulder, arm, kara (hand), fingers, nails; 15.119: ṭa-vargas on kaṭi (hip), thigh, etc. The wheel's NYĀSA ring follows this literally — that is why it says hand/fingers/nails and hip. Apparatus (Dyczkowski vol 8, App. A pp.354–355 + App. C p.374, number-paired mantra↔locus): shoulder, arm, elbow, wrist, fingers of the hand; buttock/thigh/knee/shank/toes. The site map now follows the verse (v1 canonical); the apparatus survives only as a recorded per-phoneme variant, never used for install. Same split on a: verse lalāṭa (forehead, TĀ 15.117) vs table locus 1 = Topknot. Readout below shows the apparatus variant wherever it differs. Standalone wheel · Site body maps
