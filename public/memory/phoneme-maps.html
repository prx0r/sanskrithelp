Accuracy note

Tradition-specific practice coordinates (Flood: entextualisation) — not biomedical anatomy, not a universal chakra chart. Mātṛkā = TĀ 15 limb map. Mālinī body = MV 3.37–41 (Pradīpaka / TĀ 15 apparatus). Yoginī names vary across sources; body loci are the stable core.

1 · Mātṛkā — SVG body map

Vowels head/face · ka-varga right upper · ca-varga left upper · ṭa/ta lower · pa-varga torso · deep constituents after.

3 · Mālinī — procession na → pha

Mixed vowels/consonants (bhinna-yoni). Install for śākta-śarīra only after ordinary order is stable.

Mātṛkā = articulated order. Mālinī = Śakti’s reconfiguration — same total field, permuted. Master Mātṛkā first.
